# Process icon sources

- `codex.svg`: [Lobe Icons Codex.Color](https://icons.lobehub.com/components/codex), SVG supplied by the user. The white app tile and web presentation attributes were removed; the gradient mark is unchanged and its dynamic gradient ID was replaced with a stable local ID. MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub. See `LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `opencode.svg`: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), static SVG package `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.1. MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub. See `LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `pi-agent.svg`: [Pi Agent](https://pi.dev), SVG supplied by the user. Web presentation and accessibility attributes were removed, and the 470-unit artwork was uniformly scaled into a 24-unit viewport; the three original colors are unchanged.
- `claude-code.svg`: [VSCode Icons `file_type_claude.svg`](https://github.com/vscode-icons/vscode-icons/blob/master/icons/file_type_claude.svg). Its 32-unit artwork is uniformly scaled to 24 units and filled white by the card renderer. MIT License, Copyright (c) 2016 Roberto Huertas. See `VSCODE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `python.svg`, `bunjs.svg`, `git.svg`, `go.svg`, `rust.svg`, and `zig.svg`: [VSCode Icons](https://github.com/vscode-icons/vscode-icons), via `@iconify-json/vscode-icons` 1.2.87. MIT License; see `VSCODE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`. The SVG viewports were scaled from 32 to 24; their artwork and colors are unchanged. Attribution: VSCode Icons contributors.
- `nodejs.svg`: [SVG Logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos), `logos:nodejs-icon-alt` via `@iconify-json/logos` 1.2.15, CC0 1.0. The icon was uniformly scaled and centered in a 24 unit SVG viewport.
- `terminal-mark.svg`, `git-diff.svg`, `file.svg`, `browser.svg`, and `settings.svg`: Eggie artwork.
